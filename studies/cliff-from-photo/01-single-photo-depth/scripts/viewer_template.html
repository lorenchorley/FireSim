What am I looking at?

A flat sheet of the photo, draped over a depth map that AI models guessed from the picture alone. It looks right from where the photo was taken and gets stretched or empty as you move away. Nothing here is measured.

Sizes assume the cliff face is about 600 m from the camera. Every distance scales with that guess. The camera's field of view (60°) is also an AI estimate.

Depth strength: 100% is the depth the AI guessed. Lower values flatten the sheet toward a poster. The Photo view never changes.

This copy was built without the photo, so Front/back only shows which side of the sheet you are looking at.

Clay shows the shape without the photo. Agreement colours the sheet green where three different depth models agree and red where they don't. Invented detail adds small grooves where the photo has thin dark lines; it is made up, not measured.
