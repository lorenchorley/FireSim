# D-CONTRACT-070-detail-looks: the detailed streaks' look and battery (0.7.0 item E), with F4 and F5

Scout design for the lanes of item E of COMMON-070 (base `08cf01d`, the 0.6.0 tree). Facts are from the code at the file:line given; [est] is an estimate, [open] is not known. Nothing was run on a phone or in a browser: what was run is node (unit tests by path, two cold-start probes, three pure-geometry prototypes) and is named with its command in section 8. Display only: nothing here reaches a snapshot, the engine or the history (plan section 4, rule 1); colour, direction of travel far from obstacles and speed stay the model's.

## 2. The designs, with arithmetic

### 2.1 Flow around ridges and crowns (item E1)

**What exists.** The streak is draped at `ground + max(1.5 m, 3 px) + 3 m x lift` (`line` 1035), lifted over a windward slope and sunk in the lee (`terrain()`), meandered by the shader, and drawn 40 m toward the eye in the depth test so crowns do not hide it. So it crosses crowns and ridges as if they were not there. Canopy data in the scene: (i) `FuelMap.canopyHeight`, `canopyCover` per fire cell (30 m, 20 m for a high-detail scenario; 320 kB at 6 km, held already, `SceneView.fuel` 172/398); (ii) the placed trees, `VegetationLayer.sets` (up to 60,000, 20 bytes each, held already). Neither reaches the layer today. `HeightField` of the drawn mesh (400 x 400 at High) and the 10 m ground (`sliceGround`) are both in `GlyphData`.

**New data, zero new memory.** `GlyphData.canopy?: FuelMap` (by reference; `SceneView.refreshFuel` 471 passes the new object), `WindGlyphLayer.setCrowns(src: CrownSource | null)` with `CrownSource { readonly serial: number; sets(): readonly CrownSet[] }`, `CrownSet = Pick<VegInstances, 'group' | 'count' | 'position' | 'size'>` (a view of `VegetationLayer.sets`; `serial` bumps when `pump` swaps `sets`). Both are null when the canopy layer is off (`layers.vegetation` false): a stroke does not bend round trees that are not drawn. The layer adds `crownSerial` to `BuiltPicture` / `PictureNow` and a rebuild reason `'canopy'` (`glyphSchedule.rebuildReason`, after `'height'`; `mayRebuild` still holds it to one rebuild per 250 ms, `first` and `settle` excepted). The placement settles 350 ms after the camera stops (`SceneView.frame` 1446), so a gesture costs one extra rebuild at most.

**T1: canopy-top draping (D2).** `Hc(x, y)` is the bilinear canopy height (clamped to 0..60 m, NaN as 0), `cw = clamp(cover / 0.30, 0, 1)` its bilinear cover weight (open woodland below 30 % cover keeps today's ground stroke). The stroke's plane is `zc = 0.85 x Hc x cw`; drawn height `ground + max(yoff, zc) + 3 m x lift` (the lee's `yoff` floor of 0.5 m stays). The wall test (`maxRise`, 1088) judges the GROUND only: the canopy edge is a ramp of about 20 m over one 30 m cell (slope 0.67 against `GLYPH_MAX_SLOPE` 1) and must not cut lines. Cost: 8 reads a step (two rasters x 4) [est 0.09 us] = about +1.0 ms for 11,700 steps at the High cap, warm. Skipped when `canopy` is absent.

**T2: ridge deflection (D5).** Per step, at the step's midpoint, three `ground10At` reads (the point and a forward difference of 12 m) give the gradient `g`. With `d` the unit flow direction and `s = g.d` the rise per metre along the flow: `w = smoothstep(0.10, 0.35, s)` (6 to 19 degrees), `ghat = g / |g|`, `d' = normalize(d - beta w ghat (ghat.d))`, `beta = 0.6`. Where the ground falls ahead (`s <= 0`) nothing changes (the lee sink already draws that). Every step turns at most 30 degrees (the existing "no step over 45 degrees" test stays true). Cost: 3 reads a step = about +1.2 ms at the cap [est]. Prototype `ridge.mjs` (400 m square, a ridge 35 degrees off the wind and a round hill, 841 lines of 16 steps of 3.2 m, beta 0.7 s 0.08 to 0.30): mean turn 5.6 degrees over the picture, 0 today; lines slide along the windward contours and funnel into the gap between hill and ridge, which piles them into a bundle there (physical, and visible: the density rule of 32 px is not kept in a gap; start with beta 0.6 and 0.10 to 0.35 and judge on the real picture).

**T3: crowns (D3, D4, D7).** At a rebuild, if the layer has crowns and a 5 m crown would be at least 8 px across (`pxPerMetre1 x 5 / (8 x distance) >= 1`, i.e. a camera nearer than about 580 m at 412 x 868; scales with the screen), a new phase `PHASE_CROWNS` (before `PHASE_TRACE`) scans `sets` of groups 0 to 6 (trees; not heath, understorey or grass) and keeps a tree when it is inside the box grown by one line reach plus `3 r`, its height `h >= zs + 1.5 m` where `zs` is the stroke height there, and `zs <= h` (the stroke is below the crown top); its disc radius at the stroke is `r = (width / 2) sqrt(1 - ((zs - 0.7 h) / (0.3 h))^2)`, kept when `r x pxPerM >= 4 px`. The scan reads x, y, width, height (16 bytes of the 20) and checks the clock every 4,096 trees (60,000 trees = 0.5 ms warm [est], 2 to 3 ms cold). Scratch: at most `MAX_CROWNS` = 1,024 crowns (x, y, r, flag: Float32 x 4 = 16 kB; the bound is the screen, not the forest: at 8 px a crown covers at least 800 px^2, so a 412 x 868 view plus its margin holds at most about 700) + a uniform grid of 32 m cells over the box (at most 64 x 64 `Int16` heads = 8 kB, `Int16` next 2 kB) + the eddy list of 32 sites x 6 floats (1.5 kB): **about 30 kB**, allocated once with the builder (the alloc test stays under 1 kB a rebuild).
The perturbation at a point is the sum over the crowns within `3 r` (a grid lookup touches 1 to 4 cells): the soft doublet of potential flow past a cylinder in the frame of the unperturbed wind (`x` along, `y` across, `a = 0.35 r`, `U` the wind speed): `du_along = -U r^2 (x^2 - y^2) / (x^2 + y^2 + a^2)^2`, `du_across = -2 U r^2 x y / (x^2 + y^2 + a^2)^2`, faded by `(1 - (dist / 3 r)^2)^2`. The tracer's direction is `normalize(wind + perturbation)`; the speed (colour bin, `e.x`) is the wind's. A step turns at most 30 degrees; a line stops at a rim (`dist < 0.85 r`); a seed inside `0.8 r` of a crown is skipped (counted in the new `out.blocked`). The line's pin is `pin = 1 - 0.8 max_k clamp(1 - dist_k / (2.5 r_k), 0, 1)` over its vertices, written into `aC.a` and multiplied into the shader's `turbF` (D7).
Prototype `geom2.mjs` (plan view, 70 m square, 10 px/m, lattice 3.2 m = 32 px, 14 steps of 1.1 m, crowns 3 to 7 m, uniform wind 5 m/s with a slow veer):

| cover | crowns | lines today | lines kept (around) | vertices inside a crown: today -> around | mean turn per line: today / around / with wakes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.12 | 7 | 484 | 438 (91 %) | 10.7 -> 3.1 % | 2 / 16 / 30 degrees |
| 0.35 | 22 | 484 | 359 (74 %) | 28.1 -> 8.2 % | 2 / 37 / 60 |
| 0.65 | 41 | 484 | 270 (56 %) | 43.8 -> 12.2 % | 2 / 52 / 94 |

The pictures (`shots/cov35-row2.png`, `cov12-row2.png`, `cov65-row2.png`): at cover 0.12 and 0.35 the lines part round each disc and close behind it, which reads as flow round trees; at 0.65 they crowd into the gaps and the crowns stand empty. Without the 30 degree turn cap and the stop at the rim (first run) lines tangle in stars at the stagnation point of each disc: those two rules are part of the design. Cost in the prototype (800 lines x 14 steps, cover 0.35, an allocating test harness): 338 ns a step today, 695 around, 848 with wakes: the obstacle terms cost +0.35 to +0.5 us a step unoptimised; typed arrays, a culled grid and one distance test per crown aim at +0.2 us: **+2.3 ms for 11,700 steps at the cap, +1.3 ms for a phone's 6,500** [est]. Warm builder total with T1 + T2 + T3 + eddies: about 2.4 -> 7 ms at the High cap (11,757 segments), 1.1 -> 3.5 ms for a phone picture (6,804 segments); at 4x CPU 28 ms and 14 ms, in slices of 8 ms (section 2.4's rule keeps each slice on its budget).

**Cost levers and rules.** `relief` off, no canopy, or crowns too small on the screen: each term is skipped, so the 0.6.0 cost is the floor. The builder's perf test limit of 10 ms for a whole High-cap rebuild stays; the slice p95 limit of `BUDGET_MS x 3 + 1` stays.

### 2.2 Eddies that read as swirls (item E2, D6)

**Mechanism.** The same obstacle list gives the sites; the same loop that adds the doublet adds a Rankine vortex pair, so one code path and no extra segments. For a site with obstacle radius `r` and the local wind direction `e` (across `n`): two vortices at `1.7 r e +- 0.9 r n`, core `a = 0.7 r`, tangential speed `kappa U r_v / a` inside the core and `kappa U a / r_v` outside, `kappa = 0.6`, faded to 0 at `3.2 r` (the fade starts at `2 a`), the upper one (`+n`) clockwise, the lower anticlockwise (the flow on the axis then runs back to the obstacle, the recirculation of a bluff body). A line that starts near a core curls into it; the pulse of the shader runs round the curl, so the swirl turns on screen. The meander of the lines near it is pinned (D7), so the curl is the picture and not the noise.
**Sites (a) tall crowns:** an obstacle crown with `h - zs >= 3 m` (proud of the stand) and no other obstacle within `2.2 r`, kept by a hash priority under 0.5 (a stateless test on the tree's own position, so the same tree has or has no wake in every view; no top-K over the box). **(b) ridge lee:** candidates on a FIXED world lattice of 48 m (jitter by hash of the indices, not of the zoom), kept where `terrain()` reads `lee >= 0.6`, the ground falls at least 0.25 along the flow, and the ground 60 m upwind is at least 12 m higher; `r = clamp(0.25 x the drop, 10, 40) m`; priority under 0.5. **(c) the plume:** at most two sites from `GlyphData.plumes?: readonly Plume[]` (`Plume { x, y, radiusM, strength }`; `SceneView` makes them from `fireCentroid` at the snapshot: `strength = min(1, maxIntensity / 20,000 kW/m)`, `radiusM = 30 + 90 strength`, `kappa` scaled by `strength`), the pair behind the plume's base by the wind at the fire. A site is used only when its core is at least 10 px across; `MAX_EDDIES` = 32 (a hash-ordered safety stop). All three are world data (position, wind at the site, ground), so a pan does not move a swirl.
**Size on screen.** At the closest zoom (25 px/m, a 5 m crown) a wake core is 88 px and its reach 400 px: it fills a phone's width and the lines show arcs, not full turns (a line is 90 to 220 px, 3.6 to 8.8 m; a full turn of a core of 3.5 m is 22 m). At 130 m (7.5 px/m) a line (12 to 30 m) wraps a crown-sized vortex about once. Prototype with wakes at cover 0.12 / 0.35: hooks and curls behind the isolated crowns (mean turn 30 / 60 degrees against 16 / 37 for the bend alone); at 0.65 it is a tangle, which is why sites (a) demand a free crown. **Fallback** if the lattice lines alone do not read (the real check is a picture on the closest zoom): 2 extra "eddy seeds" per site at 0.6 and 1.1 core radii, identity `birth = 99` (never part of the lattice), 8 to 24 segments each: at 12 eddies that is at most 576 segments, 4.8 % of the High cap and 14 % of the Low cap, so 6 eddies at Low.
**Honesty.** The sheet says the bends and eddies are added for looks (section 3). The colour bin is the model's speed at the segment's middle (`sp2` of the unperturbed field): the existing colour test is run with `relief` on. `e.x` (the line's speed, which sets pulse rate and tail) stays the speed at the seed.

### 2.3 Stop the animation after a minute without a touch (item E3, D9 to D11)

**How the app decides to render.** `renderer.setAnimationLoop` calls `frame(now)` every display frame (373); `frame` returns early (1430-1433) unless `dirty`, the camera `moved`, or one of the `animating` terms is true (1417-1429), and caps pure animation at 30 fps (1435-1437). Today `animating` is true in a default forest scene because of the sway term (`windSway: true`, `layers.ts:141`; wind over 0.5 m/s; trees planned) and true with the Wind streaks on (default), a fire (flames, embers) or spot fires, so a phone left on a map draws 30 frames a second for ever. Stopping the streaks alone would stop nothing in a forest. **The scope is therefore all ambient motion:** the shader time `uClock` drives the detailed streaks, the tree sway, the flames, the terrain's fire flicker and marker pulses and the smoke (`grep uClock`: `terrainLayer`, `treeShaders`, `fireLayer`, `smokeLayer`, `iconLayer`, `placesLayer`, `detailBatch`); the CPU particles (`wind.animate`, `embers.animate`) advance by the render dt.
**Design.** New pure `src/render/idleStop.ts`: `IDLE_STOP_MS = 60_000`; `class IdleClock { constructor(limitMs); poke(nowMs): boolean /* true when this poke ended an idle stop */; idle(nowMs): boolean; setLimit(ms); }` (0 or `Infinity` = never) and `idleStopLimit(raw: unknown): number` for the test seam. `SceneView` owns one. In `frame(now)`: `const idle = this.activity.idle(now)`; (1) `if (!idle) uClock += dt` (frozen while idle: an incidental frame, for example a vegetation re-plan finishing, redraws the SAME picture, so the streaks cannot creep); (2) the activity pokes (below) run before it; (3) `animating` ANDs every ambient term (`wind.animating`, `glyphs.animating`, `vegetation.animating`, flames, embers, spot fires) with `!idle`; (4) `wind.animate` and `embers.animate` are skipped when idle (their buffers keep what was last written and still draw); (5) on a change of `idle`, `this.glyphs.setPaused(idle)` and, on a wake, `dirty = true`. The first frame after a wake has `dt` capped at 0.1 s (existing, 1379), so nothing jumps. The 4 Hz chip poll and the rAF callback keep running (D11): an empty `frame()` costs microseconds; the saving is the GPU and the per-frame JS.
**What counts as activity (poke):** a `pointerdown`, `pointermove`, `wheel`, `keydown` or `touchstart` anywhere in the document (one set of capture, passive listeners on `window`, added in the constructor and removed in `dispose`); `interacting`, `moved` or `rig.flying` in `frame` (the camera is moving: also catches `flyTo` and `home`); the display time changing (`playing`, and `update(snapshot)`, which the session calls only when the view's snapshot changes); `setLayers`, `setViewMode`, `setScenario`, `refreshFuel`, `setIgnitions`, `setInsights`, `resize` (rotation), `setCovered(false)`, and the page becoming visible again (`visibilitychange`). **Not activity:** the layers' own work (a rebuild or its swap, a fade, the vegetation's placement), the chip's poll, a DOM timer. A scenario edit and a wind change reach the view as a user touch and a new snapshot, so both wake it; a simulation that is running (the head moving, the view following) pokes with every snapshot and never pauses.
**Reduced motion.** Unchanged: the streaks are frozen already, `animating` is false for them; `detail` stays `'still'` (not `'paused'`), because `setPaused` only marks a layer that is `moving`. The idle clock still runs and freezes `uClock`, which changes nothing the person can see.
**Test seam.** `SceneViewOptions.idleStopMs?: number` and the global `window.__firesimIdleStopMs` read once at construction through `idleStopLimit` (the app has no option for it). The shared `e2e/fixtures.ts` sets it to 0 (never) for every spec, because several specs wait over a minute with the view up and then expect movement; `e2e/wind-motion.spec.ts` overrides it (3,000 ms).
**Arithmetic.** At the 30 fps cap an idle minute is 1,800 frames; each is a whole scene (terrain 2 x 400^2 = 320,000 triangles, canopy up to 250,000, one detail batch of up to 24,000, at a pixel ratio up to 2) plus `frame()` JS of about 1 to 3 ms on a phone [est]: after the minute the GPU and that JS go to nothing. The battery figure is unmeasured [open]: the phone check is the battery percent over 10 minutes with the app untouched, on 0.6.0 and on this build, screen on.

### 2.4 The cold first zoom into level 4 (item E4, D12)

**What was measured here (node, a fresh process each time).** The real layer and the real builders at the High tier, the 6 km Katoomba scale (a 400 x 400 drawn mesh, a 600 x 600 10 m ground, a 45 x 45 air view), L3 at 60 px a cell then L4 at 300 px: the first frame at level 4 takes **11.2 ms**, the next four **8.1 to 8.6 ms** (the slices; the budget is 8 ms), then the swap: the first build is 5 slices and, by `--cpu-prof`, 34 ms of builder code (`line` 9.8, `walk` 7.8, `candidate` 7.5, `trace` 4.8, `step` 4.6) for 9,012 segments, against 2.4 ms warm for 11,757 segments (`detailStreaks.perf.test.ts`): **cold is 4 to 14 times warm** (a phone-size build of 6,165 segments: 19.5 and 28.3 ms cold, 3.8 to 5.1 warm; the longest gap between two clock checks of the cold builder was 1.1 to 1.3 ms, once 8.05 ms [a pause]). `lowest()` is 3.7 ms of the first frame (self time; 5.3 ms in a plain loop of 160,000 cells).
**Cause (the parts that are the layer's).** (1) `lowest(drawGround)` walks the whole drawn mesh once, at the first level-4 `begin`, although `HeightField.minElevation` was computed at scenario build: 3.7 ms at 1x, about 15 ms at 4x, in the one frame that cannot be sliced. (2) The builder is interpreted for its first rebuilds: the clock is read every 256 cells and every 4 lines in that period (`CELL_CHECK_COLD`, `base`), and a cold line costs 0.3 ms at 1x, so a slice stops up to 1.3 ms (1x) late, 5 ms at 4x: the verifier's 10 to 15 ms slices (budget 8 + up to 5 + noise). (3) The first visible frame of the detail mesh compiles and links its program (`detailBatch.ts` vertex shader: three octaves of value noise and a depth slide): unmeasured here, [est] 20 to 150 ms on a phone with ANGLE, and **not in the verifier's numbers**, because its harness stubbed `renderer.render` (`zz-v-cost.spec.ts` `drive()`). Nothing in the app compiles a program ahead (`grep compile` in `src` is empty). (4) The first upload of five instanced buffers (864 kB at the High cap, 0.3 ms [est]). **Not the layer's:** by this arithmetic the layer's own share of the first level-4 frame at 4x is about 8 ms (a slice, which stops on the clock and does not scale) + 4 x 3 ms (`lowest`, `begin`, `boxFor`) + up to 5 ms of overshoot = **about 25 ms**, against the verifier's long tasks of 52, 87 and 98 ms; the remaining 25 to 70 ms is in the same frames and is not ours (the vegetation's re-placement and level-of-detail plan at that zoom, marker refresh, other first-time code). This is an inference: the check is a Chrome trace of the first zoom with drawing NOT stubbed (below).
**Fix.** (a) `lowest(g)`: `typeof g.minElevation === 'number' ? g.minElevation : walk` (the `GroundRaster` type gets an optional `minElevation`); test with an `elevation` that throws when read. (b) In `detailStreaks.ts`: for the first two rebuilds of a builder, `CELL_CHECK_COLD` 64 cells and one line between clock reads (a clock read is 0.05 to 0.3 us; the "allocates its number" comment is about warm optimised code); the first two slices of a layer take 4 ms (`SLICE_BUDGET_MS` 8 stays): a cold picture then costs 8 to 10 frames instead of 5 and the fade-in (400 ms) hides it. Cost: 5 ms more of latency at 1x, a slice never past 4 + 0.3 ms at 1x or 4 + 1.2 ms at 4x. (c) Pre-compile: when the first `GlyphData` arrives and the Wind streaks or the arrows are on, `SceneView` calls `renderer.compileAsync(group, camera)` (three r186 `compile` visits hidden objects too, but [open] check it; if not, make the detail mesh visible with `instanceCount` 0 for the call); `compileAsync` uses `KHR_parallel_shader_compile`, so the link does not block the thread; where the extension is missing it falls back to a synchronous compile at a quiet moment (just after the scenario's first frame) instead of at the first zoom. (d) New stats `tickMaxMs` (the longest `tick()` since the last `stats()` read, from two clock reads a frame in a typed slot) so the harness can read the layer's own share.
**Rejected.** A warm-up run of the builder on a made-up scene (about 30 ms of CPU at 1x, 120 at 4x, moved to the seconds after the scenario loads, which are already the app's heaviest); precomputing the minimum in the layer constructor (the walk again); a worker (a Float32Array copy of the rasters; not worth it for 5 ms).
**Check for the verifier.** On a Chrome trace (`Tracing.start` with `devtools.timeline`, CPU 4x, a real draw, high picture tier, Katoomba 6 km fast): the first zoom L3 -> L4 from a cold page: list the long tasks (over 50 ms) with their top frames; expected after the fix: no task from `lowest`, slices under 6 ms (4 ms budget plus one cold line), the compile off the main thread or before the zoom; whatever is left of 52 to 98 ms and is not in `windGlyphLayer` / `detailStreaks` is for another lane (record it, do not fix it here).

### 2.5 F4: the old Wind streaks and reduced motion (D13)

`WindLayer` (192 lines) gets `setMotion(allowed: boolean)` and `get animating(): boolean` (`mode !== 'off' && !suppressed && motion`). The sheet's `STILL_LINE` already says "the streaks stand still"; `windDetailState` raises the `still` limit whenever `stats.reduced` and a Wind streaks switch is on, not only at level 4 (section 2.6). Flames, embers and smoke are the fire's own picture (where it is, how hot): left moving, said in the report and in Help (docs notes).

## 6. Risks and owner questions

**Risks.**
1. **The look is not judged yet.** The prototypes are plan-view geometry in node: no GL, no tilt, no depth, no shader, no phone, no sunlight. The numbers in `DETAIL_RELIEF` are first guesses; the lane must look at real pictures before it keeps them.
2. **D2 changes every forest scene at level 4,** not only the crown ones: the streaks lift 15 to 25 m onto the canopy. If the owner prefers streaks at the ground, D3 and the crowns go with it (the plan disc at the trunk is then 10 to 20 m from the drawn blob at 35 degrees); T2 (ridges) is independent and can ship alone.
3. **Thick forest empties the picture** (D4): 56 % of the lines at cover 0.65, fewer than that on a tall stand seen at a low tilt. A compensation (the lattice has no spare seeds: `thin` already runs 1/4 to 1) is not designed; the closest zoom still has `DETAIL_MIN_SEGMENTS`.
4. **Lines change near crowns when the forest re-places** (350 ms after the camera stops): local, once per gesture; the lines are not view-dependent.
5. **Cost.** About +4.5 ms warm at the High cap, +2.3 ms for a phone picture, 4x on a phone [est]; the cold first build adds the JIT penalty to that. Levers: skip crowns at over 580 m, `MAX_CROWNS`, the kill switch, `DETAIL_RELIEF.crowns.reachFactor`.
6. **Swirls read as noise** if they are many or in thick forest: only proud crowns, a free crown, 32 at most, hash priority 0.5; the fallback of dedicated eddy seeds is in 2.2 and costs segments.
7. **The shader pin** reads `aC.a`: any code that writes a colour alpha of 0 for a segment (none today) would now mean "drop"; the tests pin that.
8. **Idle stop freezes the fire's flicker and the marker pulses too** (`uClock`): a projector in a classroom looks hung after a minute. The wake is any touch; a running simulation never pauses. Owner Q3.
9. **`compileAsync` and hidden meshes** [open]: three r186's `compile` traversal and the extension on the target WebView. The fallback is a synchronous compile at a quiet moment.
10. **The e2e fixture seam** (`__firesimIdleStopMs`) is a global read in production code, like `?debug` and `?mock`; a spec that forgets the fixture would see an idle stop after a minute.
11. **Reduced-motion scope.** Sway and the old streaks stop; flames, embers, smoke and marker pulses do not (they are the fire and the markers). A reader of WCAG 2.3.3 may want pulses stopped too: owner Q4.
12. **Budget.** All JS +5 to 6 kB of the 19 kB left, shared with the other 0.7.0 lanes (E1b, E2, speed test, wind direction follow-ups): the lane reports the measured kB; `detailObstacles.ts` is in the lazy render chunk.
13. **A `Float32Array` canopy by reference** assumes `refreshFuel` always passes the new object (471-476); a fuel edit that mutates canopy in place without `refreshFuel` would leave a stale rebuild: the `'canopy'` reason is not raised by it (canopy height is not editable today).

**Owner questions (defaults in section 0).**
- **Q1.** May the streaks ride on the canopy top in forest (15 to 25 m up) instead of at the ground? Default yes: it is what makes "around the crowns" true on screen. If no: ridges only, and the crown request is dropped or redesigned as the thin trunk-scale flow.
- **Q2.** In thick forest the streaks keep to the gaps and the crowns stay empty (the owner's "around, not over" taken literally). Default yes; the alternative is to keep a share of streaks over thick stands at the canopy top.
- **Q3.** After a minute without a touch the WHOLE view holds still (streaks, sway, flames, shimmer, marker pulses), not the streaks only: the streaks alone would stop nothing in a forest or with a fire on screen. Default yes. Also: a shorter time in the Saver performance mode (for example 30 s)? Default no, 60 s for all.
- **Q4.** Reduced motion: do marker pulses and flame flicker stop too? Default no (information), sway and the old streaks yes.
- **Q5.** Should "paused to save battery" be shown on the map, not only in the sheet, the Layers line and the debug row? Default no (nothing pops up by itself).
